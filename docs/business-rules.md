# Business Rules — ServiceDesk Clinic

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the business rules that govern ServiceDesk Clinic operations.

These rules establish constraints for patient registration, appointment booking, doctor schedules, token allocation, queue management, offline synchronization, and medical records.

Business rules must be enforced by the backend wherever applicable. Frontend validation alone is not sufficient.

## 2. Clinic Management Rules

### BR-001: Clinic Ownership
Every clinic-owned record must be associated with a valid clinic.

### BR-002: Clinic Data Isolation
Users must only access information belonging to their authorized clinic, according to their assigned permissions.

### BR-003: Active Clinic
New appointments and registrations must not be accepted for a clinic that has been deactivated.

## 3. User and Permission Rules

### BR-004: Authentication
Users must authenticate before accessing protected clinic functionality.

### BR-005: Role-Based Authorization
Every protected operation must verify the user's role and permissions on the server.

### BR-006: Doctor Access
Doctors may only access appointments and medical records they are authorized to view.

### BR-007: Receptionist Access
Receptionists may manage patient registration, appointment booking, and queue operations according to clinic permissions.

### BR-008: Administrative Access
Only authorized administrators may modify clinic configurations and staff permissions.

## 4. Patient Management Rules

### BR-009: Unique Patient Identifier
Every registered patient must receive a unique internal patient identifier.

### BR-010: Duplicate Detection
The system should check for potential duplicate patients before creating a new patient record.

### BR-011: Patient Record Retention
Patient records and historical consultations must not be permanently deleted through ordinary staff operations.

### BR-012: Minimum Required Information
Only information necessary for clinic operations should be collected.

## 5. Doctor and Schedule Rules

### BR-013: Active Doctor
Appointments can only be created for an active doctor.

### BR-014: Valid Schedule
Doctor schedules must have valid start and end times.

### BR-015: Consultation Duration
Appointment duration must follow the doctor's configured consultation duration unless an authorized override is explicitly supported.

### BR-016: Schedule Availability
New appointments must fall within the doctor's configured working sessions.

### BR-017: Schedule Changes
Changes to a doctor's schedule must not silently invalidate existing confirmed appointments.

## 6. Appointment Rules

### BR-018: Appointment Ownership
Every appointment must belong to exactly one patient and one doctor.

### BR-019: Valid Appointment Time
Appointment start time must be earlier than appointment end time.

### BR-020: Appointment Conflict Prevention
The system must prevent overlapping active appointments for the same doctor.

### BR-021: Cancelled Appointment
Cancelled appointments must not block future availability.

### BR-022: Server-Side Availability
The backend must validate availability when an appointment is submitted, regardless of what the frontend displays.

### BR-023: Appointment Status
Appointment statuses must be controlled by the backend and follow permitted transitions.

### BR-024: Booking Confirmation
An appointment must only be displayed as confirmed after successful server-side validation and persistence.

### BR-025: Booking Source
The system must identify whether an appointment originated from the front desk or online booking.

## 7. Token Allocation Rules

### BR-026: Doctor-Specific Queue
Each doctor must maintain an independent patient queue.

### BR-027: Token Uniqueness
A token must not be duplicated within its configured doctor, date, and session scope.

### BR-028: Token Generation
Tokens must be generated by trusted backend logic when online and by the designated offline front-desk device when operating offline.

### BR-029: Token Scope
The initial numbering policy will use a doctor-and-session-based token scope. The exact reset policy must be configurable or formally confirmed before implementation.

### BR-030: Token History
Once allocated, token history must be retained even if an appointment is cancelled or marked as no-show.

### BR-031: Concurrent Allocation
Simultaneous booking requests must not generate duplicate tokens.

## 8. Queue Management Rules

### BR-032: Queue Eligibility
Only eligible appointments may enter the active waiting queue.

### BR-033: Check-In
A patient must be checked in before being treated as physically waiting, unless an authorized workflow explicitly permits otherwise.

### BR-034: Call Next
The doctor may call the next eligible patient in the queue.

### BR-035: Skip Patient
A doctor may skip a patient without permanently removing their appointment.

### BR-036: Resume Patient
An authorized user may return a skipped patient to the queue according to the clinic's queue policy.

### BR-037: Consultation Completion
An appointment may be marked completed after the consultation workflow has been completed.

### BR-038: Queue Persistence
Queue state must persist after page refreshes and application restarts.

### BR-039: Public Display Privacy
Public waiting-room displays must show token numbers only by default. Patient names, contact details, and medical information must not be displayed.

### BR-040: Queue Event History
Important queue actions, including calling, skipping, resuming, and completing a patient, must be recorded.

## 9. Offline Operation Rules

### BR-041: Offline Device
The initial offline workflow must use one designated front-desk device as the local booking authority.

### BR-042: Local Persistence
Supported offline operations must be stored locally until successfully synchronized.

### BR-043: Pending Status
Locally stored operations must be marked as pending synchronization.

### BR-044: Unique Operation Identifier
Every offline operation must have a unique identifier.

### BR-045: Idempotent Synchronization
Retrying the same offline operation must not create duplicate records.

### BR-046: Conflict Detection
Conflicting offline and online records must be identified and handled explicitly.

### BR-047: No Silent Reordering
The system must not silently change a patient's confirmed queue position during synchronization.

### BR-048: Offline Availability
Online booking cannot be guaranteed during a complete clinic internet outage.

### BR-049: Offline Data Protection
Locally stored patient information must be minimized and protected against unauthorized access.

## 10. Notification Rules

### BR-050: Notification Independence
Failure to deliver a notification must not interrupt appointment or queue management.

### BR-051: Notification Privacy
Notifications must avoid exposing unnecessary medical information.

### BR-052: Advisory Notifications
Patient notifications are informational. Physical patient calling remains the clinic's responsibility.

## 11. Medical Record Rules

### BR-053: Consultation Ownership
Every consultation record must be associated with a valid patient, doctor, and appointment or visit.

### BR-054: Medical Record Authorization
Only authorized users may access medical records.

### BR-055: Historical Preservation
Previous consultation records must be preserved.

### BR-056: Prescription History
Prescription changes must preserve the relevant historical record rather than silently replacing previous prescriptions.

### BR-057: Doctor Responsibility
Medical notes, diagnoses, prescriptions, and treatment decisions are the responsibility of authorized medical professionals.

## 12. Laboratory and Follow-Up Rules

### BR-058: Valid Lab Request
A laboratory report submission should be associated with an appropriate test request or authorized workflow.

### BR-059: Private File Access
Uploaded laboratory reports must not be publicly accessible.

### BR-060: Report Review
Only authorized doctors may review reports and add medical remarks.

### BR-061: Physical Follow-Up
The doctor determines whether a patient requires a physical follow-up.

### BR-062: No Automated Diagnosis
The system must not independently diagnose patients or prescribe treatment.

## 13. Audit and Data Integrity Rules

### BR-063: Important Action Logging
Important administrative, appointment, queue, and medical record actions must be logged.

### BR-064: Data Validation
All critical business rules must be validated on the backend.

### BR-065: Transaction Safety
Operations involving token allocation and appointment booking must use appropriate database transaction and concurrency controls.

### BR-066: Backup and Recovery
The system must have a documented backup and recovery strategy before production use.

## 14. Future Financial Rules

Financial rules will be documented separately when the financial management module enters development.
